# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity Levels
- Sev0: data breach / prolonged outage
- Sev1: major customer impact
- Sev2: degraded non-critical features

## Response Workflow
1. Open incident channel and assign commander.
2. Triage blast radius and customer impact.
3. Apply mitigation and track timestamps.
4. Recover service and verify SLO stabilization.
5. Publish postmortem with corrective actions.
